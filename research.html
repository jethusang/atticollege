Research & Innovation

ATTI College is committed to research and innovation in technology and business education. Our faculty and students engage in projects that address real-world challenges and drive progress in our community.

Current Research Areas

  • Digital transformation in education
  • Emerging technologies in business
  • Community development through ICT
  • Entrepreneurship and small business growth
